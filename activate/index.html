Activate your account

Two quick steps. It takes about a minute.

1 Get your code on WhatsApp

Find it on your PingMe kit card or in the message PingMe sent you.

💬  Open WhatsApp & send ACTIVATE

2 Enter the code and choose a password

At least 8 characters, with a letter and a number.

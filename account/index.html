Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Does the person scanning need the PingMe app?

No. The QR opens a web page in the phone browser. The scanner does not need to install an app or sign in.

Will my phone number be shown publicly?

The public scan page is designed not to display the owner's phone number. Alerts are routed through PingMe using the configured contact channels.

Does every QR belong to a different customer?

Yes. Each registered QR uses its own unique customer/tag identifier to load the corresponding owner's alert flow.

Can I use PingMe in Tamil?

The customer scan experience supports English and Tamil.

How do I reply to the person who scanned?

You get the alert on WhatsApp. Reply to it, and your reply appears on the finder's screen. Their screen waits for your reply for about 3 minutes.

Can someone else in my family also receive the alert?

Yes. You can add a second WhatsApp number. Whoever replies first is shown to the finder, and the other person is told it has been answered.

Can I use it for pets or my child's school bag?

Yes. A PingMe QR tag works on a pet collar, a school bag, luggage, keys or a door. The finder scans it and you get the alert.

What are message credits?

Each alert sent to a WhatsApp number uses message credits. The Starter Kit includes 250. See the Credits policy in the footer for details.

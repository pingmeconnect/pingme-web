4.1 Payment verification

Orders and recharges paid through UPI/GPay or another manual payment method remain pending until Bharani Innovations verifies the transaction. Uploading a screenshot alone does not confirm payment. Customers should provide the correct transaction reference and amount.

4.2 Order cancellation

A customer may request cancellation before an order enters personalised printing, production or dispatch. Once customised QR stickers, printed products or personalised items have entered production, cancellation may not be possible except where required by applicable law or where we agree otherwise.

4.3 Damaged, defective or incorrect products

Contact us promptly with the order number and clear photographs if an item arrives damaged, defective or materially different from the order. We will review the issue and, where appropriate under applicable law and the facts, arrange replacement, repair or refund.

4.4 Digital services and credits

If a recharge is paid but credits are not added after successful verification, contact support with the transaction reference. We will investigate and correct verified errors. Unused credits, expired credits and subscription fees are handled according to the plan terms shown at purchase, subject always to applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy removes a statutory refund or remedy.

4.5 Refund method and timing

Approved refunds will normally be returned through the original payment method where feasible, or another mutually agreed lawful method. Processing time may depend on banks and payment providers. We will communicate the expected processing timeline when approving a refund.

4.6 Non-delivery or service failure

If an order is not delivered, or a paid digital service is not activated due to an issue attributable to us, contact support. We will investigate and provide an appropriate remedy in accordance with applicable law.

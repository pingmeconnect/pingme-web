6.1 Credits

Credits are account-specific and cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash unless required by law or expressly approved by Bharani Innovations. Credit deductions, delivery status and balance are shown in the customer account/history.

6.2 Delivery-based deduction

The intended model is to deduct credits for eligible messages recorded as successfully delivered, not for scans alone or messages recorded as undelivered. The actual applicable rule shown in the customer's plan and account transaction history will govern, subject to correction of technical errors and applicable law.

6.3 Expiry

Any credit or subscription expiry date must be clearly shown before purchase and in the customer account. Do not apply an expiry condition that was not disclosed at purchase.

6.4 Recharge approval

Manual UPI recharge requests are credited only after payment verification and Admin approval. A submitted screenshot is not proof of successful settlement.

6.5 Changes to pricing

Future pricing changes will apply prospectively and will be displayed before a customer places a new order or renews. A price change will not silently alter a completed purchase.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Bharani Innovations · Madambakkam, Chennai – 600 126, Tamil Nadu, India

============================================================

5.1 Delivery address

Customers must provide a complete and accurate delivery address, recipient name, PIN code and reachable mobile number. Incorrect or incomplete details may delay delivery or cause return-to-origin.

5.2 Processing time

Personalised QR products may require account/QR verification, printing, cutting, quality checks and packing. The estimated processing and dispatch time will be displayed at checkout or communicated after order confirmation.

5.3 Delivery time

Delivery estimates depend on the destination, courier partner, serviceability, weather, transport conditions and other factors beyond our reasonable control. Any delivery estimate is indicative unless expressly stated as a guaranteed service.

5.4 Tracking and delivery issues

Where tracking is available, we will share the tracking details. Contact us if the parcel is delayed, marked delivered but not received, damaged or returned. We will coordinate with the courier partner and review the available records.

5.5 Address changes

Address changes may be possible before dispatch. After dispatch, changes depend on the courier partner and may not be possible.

5.6 Shipping charges

Any shipping or handling charge will be displayed before the customer confirms the order. Do not assume shipping is free unless the checkout page expressly says so.

This Privacy Notice explains how Bharani Innovations, operating PingMe, collects, uses, stores and shares personal data when you visit pingemconnect.in, purchase a product, register an account, scan a PingMe QR code or contact us.

2.1 Information we may collect

Depending on the feature used, we may collect:

(a) customer name and mobile number;

(b) email address, if provided;

(c) delivery address and order details;

(d) account, QR-code, tag and subscription identifiers;

(e) credit balance, recharge, payment reference and transaction-verification information;

(f) scanner-submitted message, alert category and optional photo;

(g) location information, only where the scanner chooses to share it and grants device/browser permission;

(h) technical information such as device/browser type, IP/security logs, timestamps and service activity;

(i) customer support, review and referral information.

Do not send us card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords, OTPs or other secret authentication credentials. Payment proof should show only the information reasonably needed to identify the transaction. Mask unrelated account balances and personal transactions. Never upload a UPI PIN, card CVV, password, OTP, full card number or banking login details.

2.2 Why we use information

We use information as reasonably necessary to:

(a) create and manage customer accounts and QR associations;

(b) provide scanning, alert, reply and notification features;

(c) send WhatsApp or email service messages requested or enabled by the user;

(d) process orders, payments, payment verification, printing, shipping and support;

(e) manage credits, subscriptions, renewals and account history;

(f) prevent fraud, abuse, unauthorised access and technical problems;

(g) respond to complaints, legal requests and regulatory obligations;

(h) improve the security, reliability and usability of PingMe;

(i) send promotional communications only where permitted and with any consent required by law.

2.3 Location and photos

A scanner is not required to share location or a photo unless a particular feature clearly states otherwise. The browser/device should request permission before location access. If permission is denied, location may not be attached. Location accuracy depends on the device and available signals.

Photos are submitted by the scanner only when the photo feature is used. Users should avoid including unrelated people, private documents, children or sensitive information in photos. We use submitted location and photos to support the alert or interaction for which they were provided, subject to operational, security and legal retention needs.

2.4 WhatsApp and email

Where configured, alert and service information may be sent through WhatsApp/Meta or email providers. Delivery depends on the recipient's number/address, settings, provider policies, network availability and template approval. A sent notification is not a guarantee that the recipient has read or acted on it.

2.5 Sharing with service providers

We may share relevant information with service providers who help operate PingMe, such as hosting/CDN providers, database providers, email delivery providers, WhatsApp/Meta, payment providers, courier partners and technical support providers. They may process information on our behalf or under their own terms as applicable. We do not sell personal data as a business model.

2.6 Referrals and third-party information

If a customer submits a friend's name, mobile number, email or delivery details as a referral/order enquiry, the submitting customer must have the authority or permission to provide those details and should inform the friend that PingMe may contact them about the requested product or order. We will use those details for the referral/order purpose and related service communication, not unrelated marketing without an appropriate basis or consent.

2.7 Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably required for the purposes described, account and service operation, dispute handling, security, accounting, tax and other legal obligations. Some transaction and security records may need to be retained for legally required periods even after an account is closed. When data is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it, subject to legal and technical constraints.

2.8 Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information and the service. No website, network, device or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their devices and account credentials and report suspected misuse promptly.

2.8A Data incidents

If we become aware of a personal data breach, we will assess, contain, investigate and respond in accordance with the laws and rules applicable at the relevant time, including any notification duties and timelines then in force. Users should report suspected account compromise or misuse promptly using the contact details published on the website.

2.9 Your choices and requests

Subject to the provisions and commencement dates of applicable law and after verification, you may contact us to exercise privacy rights available to you, including requests for access, correction, updating or erasure where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, or raise a privacy concern. Withdrawal may affect the feature or service that depends on the information. We may need to retain certain records where law requires or permits it.

2.10 Children

PingMe accounts and purchases should be managed by adults. A child should not independently submit personal information, location or photos through PingMe. Where a feature may involve a child, a parent or lawful guardian should supervise use and provide any consent required by applicable law. Do not use PingMe to track or contact a child without lawful authority and appropriate parental/guardian involvement.

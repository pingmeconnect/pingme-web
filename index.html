Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Does the person scanning need the PingMe app?

No. The QR opens a web page in the phone browser. The scanner does not need to install an app or sign in.

Will my phone number be shown publicly?

PingMe is designed to connect the finder and owner without displaying the owner's phone number on the public scan page.

Does every QR belong to a different customer?

Yes. Each registered QR uses its own unique customer/tag identifier to load the corresponding owner's alert flow.

Can I use PingMe in Tamil?

The customer scan experience supports English and Tamil.
